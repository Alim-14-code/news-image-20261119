## The 13 cards

### 1. APEC — THREE LEADERS, ONE TABLE (Asia · Shenzhen)
Trump, Xi and Putin held the first trilateral of sitting US, Chinese
and Russian presidents — one hour at Kylin Villa, Shenzhen, nuclear
arms on top of the agenda after New START expired in February. No
deal was expected; Trump and Putin also met one-on-one, and Xi
pledged $20M for APEC cooperation.
Sources: Reuters, Al Jazeera (live, 19 Nov). Verified as HELD, not
scheduled.

### 2. THE HAGUE — MYANMAR GUILTY OF GENOCIDE (World)
The International Court of Justice ruled 9–6 that Myanmar committed
genocide against the Rohingya in the 2017 clearance operations — the
first time in the court's 80-year history it has found a state
responsible for genocide. It ordered reparations, safe return of
refugees, and investigations; ~10,000 were violently killed. Case
brought by The Gambia in 2019.
Sources: ICJ judgment, Reuters, BBC, Amnesty International (19 Nov).

### 3. BERLIN — EUROPE DEMANDS A SEAT, AND €45BN (Europe)
Leaders of Germany, France, Britain, Poland and Italy met in Berlin
with NATO chief Mark Rutte and Trump's envoys Steve Witkoff and
Jared Kushner, pressing for legally binding security guarantees for
Ukraine and for Europe to be in the room when the war's end is
negotiated. The European Commission proposed a €45bn loan for
Ukraine for 2027 — €25bn defence, €20bn budget — covering
two-thirds of Kyiv's estimated €70bn gap; it needs EU leaders' and
Parliament's approval. Britain announced new long-range drones.
Sources: Reuters (19 Nov), Euronews/AP.

### 4. TRADE — EU–INDONESIA DEAL SEALED (Europe · Brussels/Jakarta)
The EU Council authorised signature of the CEPA trade and investment
deals on 18 Nov; signing expected 21 Nov. Over 90% of products go
duty-free, 98.5% of tariff lines liberalised when fully implemented
— about €600M a year in savings — as Indonesia leaves the EU's GSP
scheme on 1 Jan 2027.
Sources: Al Jazeera (19 Nov), EU Council.

### 5. GAZA — TANKS CROSS THE YELLOW LINE (Middle East)
Israeli forces launched the first major ground operation since the
October 2025 ceasefire, west of the Yellow Line near Bureij and
Nuseirat, to destroy a tunnel described as an immediate threat.
Three Palestinians were killed. The operation was completed on
Thursday; the ceasefire remains formally in effect.
Sources: DPA, Reuters, Al Jazeera (19 Nov).

### 6. SUDAN — DRONES OVER EL OBEID: 37 DEAD (Africa)
Amnesty International called for a war-crimes probe after nine RSF
drone strikes on El Obeid (June–August 2026) killed 37 civilians
and wounded 10, hitting homes, fuel stations and the market, with
no military target nearby. The RSF denies it and blames the army.
Sources: Amnesty International (19 Nov), MENAFN.

### 7. WASHINGTON — JEFFRIES: HISTORY NEXT (Americas)
House Democrats nominated Hakeem Jeffries as speaker candidate in a
251–11 closed-door vote on 18 Nov (262 ballots cast). If elected on
the House floor on 3 Jan 2027, he would be the first Black speaker
— second in line to the presidency. The floor vote needs 218 votes.
Sources: Reuters, AP, NPR (18–19 Nov).

### 8. CRIMEA — KERCH HIT A THIRD TIME IN A WEEK (Europe)
Ukraine struck the Crimean Bridge for the third time in a week on
Thursday 19 Nov, halting trains hours before the Shenzhen
trilateral. Tuesday's strike had destroyed the central road span;
Russia said nobody was hurt in Thursday's attack and damage is
being assessed. The 19-km bridge is Europe's longest.
Sources: Reuters (19 Nov). NOTE: a NEW event on 19 Nov, distinct
from Tuesday 17 Nov's road-span collapse (see DATE CHECK).

### 9. FIJI — A NATION OUT OF WATER (Oceania · Suva)
Fiji declared a one-month national state of disaster as an El Niño
drought empties reservoirs. PM Sitiveni Rabuka set aside FJ$20M
(~$8.5M) for emergency water for families and farmers; below-normal
rain is forecast until at least April.
Sources: Reuters (19 Nov; declared 18 Nov, wire reported 19 Nov).

### 10. SICILY — THE WIND NOBODY FORECAST (Europe · Porticello)
The UK Marine Accident Investigation Branch (MAIB) published its
final report on Thursday 19 Nov: the superyacht Bayesian had a
vulnerability to high winds never recognised, because she was never
assessed at anchor with sails down and centreboard raised. A
~70-knot (80 mph) downburst — most likely, not explicitly forecast
— knocked her over in under 15 seconds; she sank 18 minutes later.
Seven died in Aug 2024, including Mike Lynch and his daughter Hannah.
Sources: The Guardian, MAIB final report (19 Nov).

### 11. CHIPS — CHINA CHASES NVIDIA'S TOP CHIPS (Americas)
The US-China Economic and Security Review Commission's annual
report to Congress (released 18 Nov, reported 19 Nov) says China's
PLA is actively seeking Nvidia's most advanced AI chips despite US
export curbs — including remote access through data centres in
third countries such as Malaysia. Huawei's Ascend and Cambricon's
Siyuan still trail Nvidia and AMD.
Sources: USCC report via Reuters (19 Nov).

### 12. APEC — PHILIPPINES–CHILE: FIRST LATAM DEAL (Asia)
The Philippines and Chile signed a free-trade agreement during a
bilateral meeting at the APEC summit in Shenzhen — the Philippines'
first such agreement with a Latin American country.
Sources: Philippine Daily Inquirer (19 Nov); Wikipedia Current
events 19 Nov.

### 13. BANGLADESH — HOMES OF EX-MPS TORCHED (Asia)
Members of the banned Awami League looted and set fire to the
ancestral home of BNP MP Md. Selimuzzaman Mollah, supporters'
houses, an unoccupied police station, parked vehicles and a Grameen
Bank branch in Gopalganj, and vandalised BNP/Jamaat-linked homes in
Kashiani; in Feni they torched the home and three trucks of jailed
ex-politician Rahim Ullah, and in Rajshahi the home of former
foreign minister Shahriar Alam.
Sources: BSS, The Financial Express, The Business Standard (19 Nov);
Wikipedia Current events 19 Nov.
