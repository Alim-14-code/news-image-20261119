# Briefs — news-image-20261119 (Thursday 19 November 2026)

All seven stories were verified against wire coverage on 19 Nov 2026

## 1. APEC — THREE LEADERS, ONE TABLE (Asia · Shenzhen)

Trump, Xi and Putin held the first trilateral of sitting US, Chinese
and Russian presidents — one hour at Kylin Villa, Shenzhen, with
nuclear arms on top of the agenda after New START expired in
February. No deal was expected; Trump and Putin also met one-on-one,
and Xi pledged $20M for APEC cooperation.

Sources: Reuters, Al Jazeera (live, 19 Nov). Verified as HELD, not
scheduled: the trilateral took place on Thursday 19 Nov; the
Trump–Putin one-on-one also happened (Kremlin aide Ushakov, via
Reuters). Xi's $20M APEC cooperation pledge reported in the live
coverage.

## 2. EU–INDONESIA — TRADE DEAL SEALED (Europe · Brussels/Jakarta)

The EU Council authorised signature of the CEPA trade and investment
deals on 18 Nov; signing is expected 21 Nov. Over 90% of products go
duty-free, 98.5% of tariff lines are liberalised when fully
implemented, 96% of Indonesian tariffs are gone within 5 years, car
duty (up to 50%) is phased out over 5 years — about €600M a year in
savings — as Indonesia leaves the EU's GSP scheme on 1 Jan 2027.

Sources: Al Jazeera (19 Nov), EU Council. Confirmed 19 Nov.

## 3. BERLIN — EUROPE DEMANDS A SEAT (Europe · Berlin)

Leaders of Germany, France, Britain, Poland and Italy met in Berlin
with NATO chief Mark Rutte and Trump's envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared
Kushner, pressing for legally binding security guarantees for Ukraine
after any ceasefire — and for Europe to be in the room when the war's
end is negotiated. Britain announced new support including long-range
drones.

Sources: Reuters, 19 Nov ("Ukraine's European allies meet in Berlin
in show of support", James Mackenzie). The meeting is the "Coalition
of the Willing" session held on Thursday 19 Nov.

## 4. FIJI — A NATION OUT OF WATER (Oceania · Suva)

Fiji declared a one-month national state of disaster as an El Niño
drought empties reservoirs. PM Sitiveni Rabuka set aside FJ$20M
(~$8.5M) for emergency water for families and farmers; below-normal
rain is forecast until at least April.

Sources: Reuters, 19 Nov ("Fiji declares state of disaster amid
El Nino-driven drought"). Declaration announced by Rabuka in a
national address on Wednesday 18 Nov; wire reported 19 Nov 01:09
UTC (08:09 WIB).

## 5. JEFFRIES — 251–11: HISTORY NEXT (Americas · Washington DC)

House Democrats nominated Hakeem Jeffries as their speaker candidate
in a 251–11 closed-door vote on 18 Nov (262 ballots cast, including
incoming members and delegates; 2 not voting). If elected on the
House floor on 3 Jan 2027, he would be the first Black speaker — and
second in line to the presidency. The floor vote needs 218 votes;
Democrats hold at least 257 seats.

Sources: Reuters, AP, NPR (18–19 Nov). Vote held 18 Nov, reported
across 19 Nov.

## 6. KERCH — THIRD STRIKE IN A WEEK (Europe · Crimea)

Ukraine hit the Crimean Bridge for the third time in a week on
Thursday 19 Nov, halting trains hours before the Shenzhen trilateral.
Tuesday's strike had destroyed the central road span; Russia said
nobody was hurt in Thursday's attack and damage is being assessed.
The 19-km bridge is Europe's longest.

Sources: Reuters, 19 Nov ("Ukraine strikes Crimean Bridge for third
time in a week"). NOTE: this is a NEW event on 19 Nov, distinct from
Tuesday 17 Nov's road-span collapse (see DATE CHECK).

## 7. BAYESIAN — THE WIND NOBODY FORECAST (Europe · Porticello, Sicily)

The UK Marine Accident Investigation Branch (MAIB) published its
final report on Thursday 19 Nov: the superyacht Bayesian had a
vulnerability to high winds that was never recognised, because she
was never assessed at anchor with sails down and centreboard raised.
A ~70-knot (80 mph) downburst — most likely, and not explicitly
forecast — knocked her over in under 15 seconds; she sank 18 minutes
later. Seven died in Aug 2024, including Mike Lynch and his daughter
Hannah.

Sources: The Guardian, MAIB final report (19 Nov). The finding
contrasts with Italian prosecutors, who have reportedly blamed crew
errors.
