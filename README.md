# news-image-20261119 — "The World in One Page"

make.fun daily news-image competition, $250 pool, top 50 paid.
Entries close 21 Nov 2026 07:00 WIB. Built 19 Nov 2026 (evening WIB)
in this repo dir, in one session, from scratch.

## The page

`entry.png`, 1024×1024, ~514 KB (limit 1 MiB). One page, Thursday
19 November 2026: **7 stories, 4 regions** (Europe 4, Asia 1,
Americas 1, Oceania 1).

- **2 lead cards** — APEC trilateral (held 19 Nov, Shenzhen) and the
  EU–Indonesia trade deal (CEPA signature authorised 18 Nov).
- **3 medium cards** — Berlin "Coalition of the Willing", Fiji's
  drought disaster, Jeffries' 251–11 speaker nomination.
- **Dotted world map** ("WHERE IT HAPPENED") from real Natural Earth
  110m land polygons, with numbered pins 1–7 for the stories.
- **2 small cards** — the Crimean Bridge's third strike in a week
  (Thursday 19 Nov) and the MAIB Bayesian final report.
- **Numbers strip** — 3 leaders · 98.5% · €600M/yr · 251–11 ·
  FJ$20M · 70 kt.
- **DATE CHECK footer** — names what was deliberately left out
  because it was first reported earlier (Waymo freeway pause,
  Tuesday's Kerch road-span collapse, Nvidia's quarter — all 17 Nov).
- **Sources line** — Reuters · Al Jazeera · The Guardian · AP/AFP ·
  MAIB · EU Council.

## Art

- Cards 1–4: AI-generated scenes in `assets/` (APEC, EU–Indonesia,
  Berlin, Fiji). The Fiji source was a `.webp`; it was converted to
  PNG in-session (`assets/card-fiji.png`). The `.json` sidecars in
  `assets/` are metadata only and are NOT committed.
- Cards 5–7: original flat illustrations drawn with Pillow
  primitives in `render_news19.py` (Capitol + 251 tally, bridge deck
  with a gap + smoke, heeled yacht in a squall), in the same muted
  palette as the AI art.

## How it was built

- `render_news19.py` — the whole page, written from scratch in this
  session (layout, text, map, illustrations). Run it to regenerate
  `entry.png`. It reads Natural Earth land polygons from
  `/tmp/ne_110m_land.geojson` at render time (downloaded in-session,
  not committed); without that file it falls back to a graticule-only
  map. No network, no clock, no randomness — two runs are
  byte-identical.
- `main.py` — stdlib only; copies `entry.png` to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`).
- `briefs.md` — the 7 briefs with sources and verification notes,
  including why the draft's Waymo story was replaced by the Kerch
  third strike.
- `entry.png` — the rendered page (generated by running
  `render_news19.py` in this session, never copied from elsewhere).

## Verification

- Every story was checked against wire coverage on 19 Nov 2026
  (see `briefs.md`). One replacement was made on verification
  grounds: Waymo (first reported 17 Nov) → Kerch third strike
  (Thursday 19 Nov).
- Determinism: `render_news19.py` run twice → identical sha256;
  `main.py` run twice into two dirs → identical output.
- Layout reviewed as an image: all text inside its cards, footer
  complete, no overlaps (fixed: EU–Indonesia deck overflow, Jeffries
  "251" colliding with its number badge).
